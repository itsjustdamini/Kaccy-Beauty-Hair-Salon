Meet Ezike Judith Kenechi

Behind every style at Kaccy Beauty Hair Salon is Ezike Judith Kenechi, the founder and lead stylist. With 13 years of experience as a hairdresser, she has braided, twisted and styled everything from simple cornrows to bold Fulani and boho looks.

Her promise is simple: every client leaves feeling like royalty, with neat parts, comfortable braids and a style that lasts. Whether it is for work, school, a wedding or just because, she takes the time to get it right.

Find us on Ifite Road in Awka, between First Market and Ukwu Aki. Walk in, or message us on WhatsApp to book your seat.
